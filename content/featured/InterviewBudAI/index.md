An open-source, local-first coach for data structures and algorithms interview prep, built from my own practice. It tracks the problems you've solved, quizzes you on them with AI, and shows which topics need work next. Bring your own model (Claude, any OpenAI-compatible server, or a local one through Ollama or Docker Model Runner); your notes never leave your machine.
